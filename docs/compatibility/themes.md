# Themes compatible with Fluid Checkout

Fluid Checkout has explicit compatibility with 150+ plugins and 90+ themes, and most other plugins and themes work out of the box.

Keep the theme you already use. Fluid Checkout gives your customers a checkout that is easy for first-time buyers and faster for repeat purchases.

Some themes change WooCommerce in their own way, so we test popular themes and add extra support where they need it. The themes below are the ones with explicit compatibility code in Fluid Checkout Lite and Fluid Checkout PRO together. The list includes free and premium themes.

Some themes ship with a companion plugin, such as Astra Pro, GP Premium, or Blocksy Companion Pro. Those plugins are listed under [Theme companion plugins](/compatibility/plugins/#theme-companion-plugins).

## When your theme is not listed

If your theme is not on this list, it can still work. The list is the themes we have explicit compatibility code for. Most other themes work out of the box.

Some themes need small adjustments when they do not use the standard WooCommerce hooks or styles, and we are happy to look at yours.

Try Fluid Checkout on your site. If anything looks off, contact us through the [support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/fluid-checkout/) and we will help you fix it.

Looking for plugins? See [Plugins compatible with Fluid Checkout](/compatibility/plugins/).

Using the WooCommerce Checkout block? See [WooCommerce Checkout and Cart blocks](/compatibility/woocommerce-blocks/).
